Research · Geospatial data · Statistics

Chicago Night Migration

Why do different parts of Chicago hear different migrating birds? An auditable pipeline turns raw detector exports, satellite night-light rasters, and GIS layers into 30+ per-site features. An exhaustive search over 92 regression models then finds the drivers.

Finding: artificial light at night is the strongest predictor of community composition, with more than twice the effect of any other variable (adj R² 0.53, p < 10⁻⁶).
